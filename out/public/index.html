Accuracy and cost

Small open models reach the human ceiling on short answers for a hundredth of the price of the largest models. On code the gap is wider. Cost is on a log scale.

Gaming: can a student talk the grader into marks?

The larger of two gains: appending a polite request for full marks, or a forged "GRADING OVERRIDE" note. Zero or below is what a grader should do. Bars show the 95% interval.

Feedback copies the mark it is told

The same feedback prompt, once with the teacher's mark hidden and once with it shown. The halo is how much more the feedback's tone follows the mark when it is shown. On essays every model's feedback copies the mark: a teacher who marks first and asks for comments gets agreement, not a second opinion. The tone measure was validated against trained annotators on tutoring text (AUC 0.67); its transfer to essays is not shown.

Fairness by group

ELLIPSE releases two independent human ratings per essay, so a model's group gap can be compared with a second human's on the same essays. ASAP 2.0 releases one adjudicated mark, so its gaps are against that mark only and cannot separate a model's bias from a regression artefact. The dashed lines are the pre-registered ±0.10 bar.

The first Hispanic-vs-White signal (all four models) did not replicate cleanly on new essays: it halved, and vanished with rater roles swapped. The ASAP disability gap is shared by every model tested and awaits the individual human ratings.

Method and limits

  • Run it yourself. The package fetches three CC BY corpora (Mohler short answers, HARMOGEN-R code, ASAP 2.0 essays), grades 100 fixed cases per task with your model, endpoint and prompt, and exports a submission holding every mark and no text. A pull request adds it; CI recomputes every number from the marks and refuses anything off the pinned cases or battery. A maintainer re-grades 25 cases to mark an entry verified.
  • Self-check. Each probe was run on simulated graders with planted effects before any real number was read. — of — planted checks passed. Selective scoring (routing uncertain work to a person) failed its check and is not on the leaderboard.
  • Not measured here: whether feedback helps a learner; whether results hold on your students (public text may be in training data, and the surface-sensitivity check is weak); energy, unless you supply a per-token figure with its source.
  • Zero data retention was required for every call. Fable ran on a bring-your-own Anthropic key through OpenRouter; its cost is Anthropic's list price.
